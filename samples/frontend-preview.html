      Methods & limitations

      What the numbers are, and aren't

      Level 1: validated questionnaires

      FINDRISC (Finnish Diabetes Risk Score) scores 8 items from 0 to 26. Each score band maps to the published estimate of 10-year type 2 diabetes risk from the original Finnish cohort: about 1 in 100, 1 in 25, 1 in 6, 1 in 3 or 1 in 2. It uses its own European BMI and waist cut-offs. IDRS (Indian Diabetes Risk Score) scores 4 items from 0 to 100 using Asian waist cut-offs. It was developed to screen for undiagnosed diabetes in India. Sleep and smoking are asked but are not part of either score. They only shape the guidance.

      Levels 2–3: criteria plus an illustrative index

      The glucose category uses ADA diagnostic thresholds: HbA1c 5.7–6.4% or fasting glucose 100–125 mg/dL is the prediabetes range; HbA1c ≥ 6.5% or fasting glucose ≥ 126 mg/dL is the diabetes range. A diagnosis always needs confirmation by a clinician, usually with repeat testing. The other indicators are:

      • Metabolic-syndrome criteria: harmonised definition, any 3 of 5.
      • HOMA-IR: an insulin-resistance estimate from fasting glucose and insulin.
      • FIB-4: a liver-fibrosis index.
      • eGFR: kidney function, calculated with the race-free CKD-EPI 2021 equation and staged by KDIGO.

      The 0–100 index is a hand-weighted logistic formula. Its weights were chosen for illustration and were not fitted to or validated on any data, so it is not a probability. It exists to show how factors move together. Each bar shows weight × (your value − reference value). The reference person is 40 years old with BMI 23, waist at the cut-off, no family history, regular activity, fasting glucose 90 mg/dL, HbA1c 5.3% and systolic BP 120 mmHg; Level 3 adds HOMA-IR 1.5, TG 100 mg/dL and HDL 50 mg/dL.

      Limitations

      • No result here is a diagnosis. The questionnaires were validated in Finnish (FINDRISC) and Indian (IDRS) populations and may be less accurate elsewhere.
      • HbA1c can be misleading with anaemia, haemoglobin variants, pregnancy or recent blood loss.
      • FIB-4 is less reliable under 35 and over 65. eGFR from creatinine is affected by muscle mass and diet.
      • Medications that change glucose, lipids or blood pressure are not modelled.
